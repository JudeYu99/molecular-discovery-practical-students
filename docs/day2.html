Activities and questions

Activity 1

Assess a targetable pocket

Open ProteinsPlus and use DoGSite3 for the c-Abl structure. Compare the highest-ranked predicted pockets with the known ligand site.

Record: pocket location, size, enclosure, hydrophobic character and one limitation of the prediction.
Activity 2

Set a focused docking box

Use the supplied centre and size as a starting point. Move and resize the orange box while checking the X, Y and Z directions.

Check: the box covers the ligand site with enough space for the complete ligand.
Activity 3

Compare two ligands

Run imatinib first. Download its results. Return to the input step, choose caffeine, and rerun with exactly the same focused settings.

Record: best score, chosen pose and whether each ligand occupies the expected pocket.
Activity 4

Compare focused and blind docking

Run imatinib with a whole-protein box. Compare the predicted site, score and calculation time with the focused search.

Think: did the blind search recover the known site, and what sampling limitations remain?
Activity 5

Inspect interactions

Use DockLab to select a pose. Open ContactCanvas with the matching protein PDB and individual pose_N.sdf file.

Compare: residue contacts, interaction types and geometry for imatinib and caffeine.
Activity 6

Minimise and reassess

Minimise one selected imatinib pose. Compare its 3D position and Vina score before and after, then generate both ContactCanvas diagrams.

Record: which contacts appeared, disappeared or changed geometry.
Optional activity

Search by ligand similarity

Open SwissSimilarity, enter imatinib, choose a small library and compare a 2D method with a 3D or combined method.

Record: method, library, similarity score and two candidates worth investigating.
Final discussion

Make a supported conclusion

Combine the pocket evidence, docking score, pose geometry and interaction pattern. Identify one result you trust and one uncertainty.

Remember: docking scores and geometric contacts are hypotheses rather than measurements of affinity or activity.

Files to keep

  • The completed student results worksheet.
  • The imatinib and caffeine DockLab result bundles.
  • A PNG showing one selected docked pose.
  • ContactCanvas diagrams for both ligands.
  • The minimised imatinib pose and before-and-after interaction diagrams.

Interpret the comparison carefully

Caffeine is included to give a clear teaching contrast. It is not a validated inactive control for c-Abl. It is also much smaller than imatinib, so size contributes to the difference in docking score and interaction count.
