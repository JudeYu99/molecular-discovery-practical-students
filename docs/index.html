Before you arrive

Bring a charged laptop with PyMOL installed. You will also need a Google account to run the Colab notebooks.

Day 1 · Protein structures and molecular recognition

Inspect a protein–ligand complex, explain plausible interactions and compare the known site with predicted pockets.

What we will do

  • Understand the targetCompare experimental and predicted structures using UniProt, PDB and AlphaFold Database.
  • Explore structures in PyMOLNavigate, change representations, select a binding site, measure contacts, superpose structures and save figures. Electrostatics and mutagenesis are short demonstrations.
  • Inspect ligand recognitionRetrieve the ligand's chemical identity, then compare your PyMOL interpretation with PLIP, PoseView and ContactCanvas.
  • Compare pharmacophore ideasIdentify structure-based features and use LigandLab to explore similarity and shared ligand features.
  • Assess binding pocketsUse ProteinsPlus and DoGSite3 to compare predicted pockets with the known ligand site.
